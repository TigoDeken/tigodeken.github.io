Modular Refillable Packaging

Problem: High material waste in single-use cosmetic containers requires a structural redesign for longevity and end-user simplicity.

Manufacturing Constraint: Injection molding tolerances. Required modeling precise screw threads, internal ribbing for structural integrity, and flush flip-top caps within wall thickness limits.

Parametric Voronoi Structures

Problem: Standard geometric lamps lack material efficiency and organic aesthetic integration.

Manufacturing Constraint: Additive manufacturing overhang limits and structural load balancing around E27 fixture fittings.

Solution: Developed custom algorithmic definitions in Python and Grasshopper to generate 3D-printable lamp bases with organic Voronoi skins, minimizing material usage while maintaining structural rigidity.

Mechanical Tuning & Component Design

Manufacturing Constraint: Real-world physics, material fatigue, and structural load on dynamic vehicles.
